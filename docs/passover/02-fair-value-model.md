# Passover 02 — The Fair-Value / Opening-Price Model

Source of truth: `docs/opening-price-model.md` (spec) + `docs/fv-research-log.md` (the full
experimental record — every test, why it ran, what it found). Implementation:
`nba_stock_market/opening_prices.py` (`ProjectedWarModel`). Output consumed by the app and
listing: `output/opening-prices-2026-27.csv` (top 300 players).

## The formula (v2, production)

```
blend         = 0.40·z(EPM) + 0.35·z(prior WAR) + 0.15·z(minutes) + 0.10·z(25 − age)
projected_WAR = 1.4821 + 2.5605 × blend        (OLS fit, outcome-isolated per window)
fair_value    = $1.2M + projected_WAR × $5M    (min salary + market price of a win)
listing       = clamp(0.9 × fair_value + 0.1 × actual_salary, $2M, $70M)
```

In English: estimate how many wins the player produces next season, convert wins to dollars
at the league's real ~$5M/win rate, anchor 10% to his actual contract for legibility.

## Why each piece (each earned its seat by measurement)

- **EPM core**: beat/matched LEBRON on the same 300-player cohort in all four backtest
  windows; agrees most with DARKO (ρ 0.83). Adding LEBRON to EPM made it worse.
- **Prior WAR + minutes**: a rate-only price LOST to naive "carry last season's WAR" every
  year — role size is a coach's decision a per-possession rating can't see.
- **Youth (small)**: +0.01-0.02 ρ consistently; kept at 10% to avoid double-counting the
  dividend economy's improver rewards.
- **The WAR fit**: makes the price *calibrated*, not just correlated — slope vs reality
  ≈ 0.9 (near the perfect-forecast diagonal) vs 0.67 for last-WAR pricing, which
  systematically overprices the top (ignores regression to the mean).
- **$1.2M + $5M/win**: standard sports-economics derivation (~$4.5B above-replacement
  payroll ÷ ~880 marginal wins); independently reproduces our hand anchors (avg ≈ $11M,
  Jokić ≈ supermax); self-updates as the cap grows.
- **90/10 salary blend**: heavier salary weights monotonically destroyed accuracy in the
  exploratory sweep (salary-only ρ 0.56); 10% is flavor at ~zero cost.

## Validation (the honest version)

Protocol: prices built from season N−1 only, scored by Spearman ρ against realized season-N
WAR, four season pairs, **cohort defined by the train season with washouts counted as zero
WAR** (survivorship-bias fix, 2026-07-14 correction). Result: FV component 0.752 / 0.773 /
0.768 / 0.655, beating the same-cohort naive baseline (mean 0.699) in **every** window.
These are model-selection windows, not untouched holdouts — the weights sit on a broad
plateau; defend the structure, not the digits. Canonical evidence: `output/fv-validation.md`
(generated by `fv_validation.py`; never trust prose numbers over the generated report).

Known limits: ~53% top-30 recall for every formula tested (star outcomes are genuinely
hard — that residual is the game); the $70M cap destroys rank information among the very
top (accepted legibility trade); rookies are absent by construction.

## What FV is FOR (important framing)

Under Option B, dividends are ~zero-expectation for a correctly-projected player *by
design* — so FV is **not** a payout-prediction machine (none can exist in a calibrated
surprise economy; we measured that). FV's jobs: fair listings, a trading anchor, and the
fair-value-vs-salary "surplus" content angle. The market and dividends do the rest.

## Pipeline & rebuild

```
python scripts/prepare_fv_inputs.py     # materializes pinned inputs (hash-verified)
python -m nba_stock_market.opening_prices   # writes output/opening-prices-2026-27.csv
```

Inputs pinned by `data/manifests/fv-inputs.json`; byte-exact copies committed under
`data/snapshots/fv/`. `--refresh` checks provider drift explicitly. EPM requires
`DNT_API_KEY`; DARKO (free public sheet, ρ 0.83 agreement) is the designated fallback
metric if D&T access lapses.
