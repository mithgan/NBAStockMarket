## Takeaway

Trailing form largely self-cancels and promotes rookies and improvers, while a fixed projection makes sustained production above expectations accumulate and brings stars back into the top earners. The salary placeholder also changes the economy from mild deflation to mild inflation and meaningfully increases Jokic's Christmas payout. Its linear salary curve caps low, so many good players beat it too easily; DARKO or Dunks & Threes projections are the real fix.
